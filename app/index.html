    Questions

    Is my memory stored on-chain?

    No. Only a 32-byte SHA-256 commitment of your encrypted state is written on-chain. The state vector itself never leaves your device unless you export it.

    What makes a Continuum old?

    The genesis anchor block. It is bound to a Solana slot and cannot be back-dated. Continuums created in 2026 keep real, verifiable seniority forever.

    Can I restore after years of dormancy?

    Yes. Import your exported Continuum file, enter the passphrase, and the state vector decrypts to the exact moment of the last checkpoint.

    What happens if the protocol website disappears?

    The site is a static, open-source page. Anyone can re-host it. Your Continuum lives in your export file and on-chain, not on our servers — there are no servers.
